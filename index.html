Simyer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Simyer ("the App") is a simple audio player application designed to allow users to play audio files stored on their device.

Information We Collect

Simyer does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from users.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or location.

Audio Files and Device Storage

Simyer may request permission to access audio files on your device. This permission is used only to allow the App to find and play audio files that you choose to access through the App.

Your audio files remain on your device. Simyer does not upload, transmit, or share your audio files with us or with third parties.

Permissions

The App may request access to audio files stored on your device. This permission is necessary for the core functionality of the App.

Third-Party Services

Simyer does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Children's Privacy

Simyer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Because Simyer does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal user data stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
